How solar works — simply

No jargon. Four things happen on your roof every day:

  1. 1

    Sunlight hits your panels. Every hour of daylight is free electricity waiting to be captured.

  2. 2

    The inverter converts it. Panel power becomes normal plug power for your appliances.

  3. 3

    Batteries store the surplus. Extra daytime power keeps you running at night and during outages.

  4. 4

    The grid becomes a backup — or a buyer. With net metering, the power company pays you for what you don't use.

Common questions

How long does an installation take?+

A typical home system takes 1–2 days. Commercial and industrial projects run on a phased schedule agreed during design — usually 1–4 weeks including commissioning.

How accurate is the online estimate?+

It's a reliable ballpark from real engineering rules — typically within 10–15% of the formal quote. A site visit confirms roof space, wiring and supply before we commit a price.

Can I start small and expand later?+

Yes — and we recommend it when budgets are tight. We design every system to scale: add panels or batteries later without replacing the core.

What warranties do you offer?+

Panels carry up to 25-year manufacturer warranties, inverters and lithium batteries per manufacturer terms, plus our own workmanship warranty on every installation.

Do you handle grid-tied and net metering?+

Yes — we design export-capable systems and help with the utility paperwork. If you're exporting power, we can also manage your metering records.
